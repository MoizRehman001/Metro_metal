Welcome to Metro Metal Impex

Since 1972, Metro Metal Impex has been a pioneering force in the export industry, specializing in high-precision fuel injection systems and diesel engine spare parts. With over five decades of experience, we have established ourselves as a trusted leader in the global market, renowned for our unwavering commitment to quality and excellence.

At Metro Metal Impex, we pride ourselves on being fuel injection specialists. Our extensive range of products includes advanced fuel injection systems and premium-quality diesel engine spare parts. Each component is crafted with meticulous attention to detail and precision engineering to meet the highest industry standards.
